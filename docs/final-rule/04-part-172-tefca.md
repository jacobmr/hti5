On April 1, 2026, the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) published a reorganization which reverses the actions that created the management title of Assistant Secretary for Technology Policy (ASTP), removing the title and role from HHS’ leadership structure, and restores the Office of the National Coordinator for Health Information Technology as a singularly titled office.57 The roles and responsibilities of the HHS Chief Technology

Therefore, we have removed all references to ASTP within part 172 of 45 CFR, and, as discussed previously, the reference in part 170 of 45 CFR. We find good cause to waive the requirement under 5 U.S.C. § 553(b) to publish a notice of proposed rulemaking in the Federal Register for these changes. We believe adherence to this requirement would be unnecessary, consistent with 5 U.S.C. § 553(b)(B) because the “Assistant Secretary for Technology Policy” no longer exists, and this finalized policy is only removing these now inaccurate and confusing references from the current regulatory text. The revisions to remove the references to ASTP within parts 170 and 172 of 45 CFR could also be considered a matter relating to agency management under 5 U.S.C. § 553(a)(2) and therefore not subject to the rulemaking requirements in 5 U.S.C. § 553.
