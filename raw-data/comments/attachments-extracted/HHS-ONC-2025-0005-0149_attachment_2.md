Dear Assistant Secretary Keane:
Health Level Seven (HL7) International welcomes the opportunity to provide feedback on the Health Data, Technology, and Interoperability: ASTP/ONC Deregulatory Actions to Unleash Prosperity Proposed Rule  (HTI-5). This letter includes perspectives from our leadership, Policy Advisory Committee, HL7 Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources (FHIR) at Scale Taskforce (FAST) and HL7 Work Groups including: Clinical Document Architecture (CDA) Management Group, Patient Empowerment, Provider/Payer Information Exchange, Public Health and Structured Documents. Our comments feature: 
Key overarching thoughts;
Remarks and recommendations on specific portions of the proposed rule; 
Observations on individual proposed certification criteria changes; and
Points regarding potential impact for HL7 Consolidated Clinical Document Architecture (C-CDA) exchange. 
Overall, HL7 strongly supports the proposed rule’s aims -- including to establish a new foundation on which to build HL7 Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources (FHIR®)-based API requirements in the future. 
Regarding this and other provisions, HL7 encourages ASTP/ONC to actively engage our organization as it finalizes and implements the Health Data, Technology, and Interoperability: ASTP/ONC Deregulatory Actions to Unleash Prosperity Proposed Rule.  
HL7 is the global authority on healthcare interoperability and a critical leader developing open standards that drive digital health innovation. As such, we stand ready to support ASTP/ONC in optimally understanding the promises, complexities and challenges of bringing this proposed rule into market reality.  While our feedback highlights significant operational risks, HL7 remains deeply committed to ASTP/ONC’s vision of a market-driven, FHIR-first ecosystem. We see our role as the bridge between that policy vision and the technical reality required.
Should you have any questions about our attached comments, please contact Rachel Dunscombe, Chief Executive Officer of Health Level Seven International at ceo@HL7.org. We look forward to continuing this discussion and offer our assistance to you on this topic that is critical to the future of our nation’s healthcare.
